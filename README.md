## Project Description

What happens when a banana gets tired of being a banana?

**Enne Kolland Irikkan Patto?** is an AI-powered object life simulator where you show us **literally any object**, and we give it a completely unnecessary life.

Your object gets a passport, goes through immigration, explores the world, makes questionable decisions, meets humans, develops a personality, faces consequences...

...and eventually **dies.**

Yes.
Not because the game ended.

Because that's how life works.

The banana becomes compost.
The phone gets replaced.
The chair gets thrown away.

Every object gets an ending.

We believe in equality.

Especially in death. 💀
---

## The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Human life is already complicated.

But objects?

Nobody is thinking about them.

Who checks whether a chair is happy?

Who asks a banana about its career goals?

Who investigates whether a pen secretly wants to become a criminal?

Nobody.

**This is the problem.**

And naturally, we solved it.

---

## The Solution (that nobody asked for)

Simply show the camera an object.

Our extremely unnecessary bureaucracy will then:

1. Identify the object.
2. Create its identity.
3. Issue it a passport.
4. Send it to immigration.
5. Interrogate it.
6. Let it enter the world.
7. Give it opportunities.
8. Let YOU make its decisions.
9. Remember everything it did.
10. Eventually kill it.

The object cannot escape Step 10.

We tried.
